Terms and Condition

In our leave management system, employees are entitled to different types of leave based on company policy and local labor laws.
Annual Leave allows employees to take paid time off for personal or vacation purposes, typically accrued based on years of service.
Sick Leave is available for employees who are ill or need medical attention and may require medical certification for extended absences.
Maternity and Paternity Leave are offered for new parents, with durations defined by law or company policy.
Compassionate Leave is provided in the event of family emergencies, such as the death of a close relative.
Employees are also entitled to Unpaid Leave if they have exhausted their paid leave entitlements or need time off for reasons not covered under paid leave.
All leave requests should be submitted and approved through the appropriate channels, following company policy for notice periods and documentation requirements.
